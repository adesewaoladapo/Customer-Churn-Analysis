# Customer Churn Analysis

## Executive Summary
An end-to-end customer retention analysis examining 6,687 subscriber records to identify root causes behind a **26.86%** overall customer churn rate.

## Core Findings
- **Competitor Poaching:** Accounted for **44.8%** of total churn ($805$ customers), driven primarily by competitor pricing promotions, device upgrades, and higher data allowances.
- **Contract Risk:** Customers on **Month-to-Month** contracts churn at **46.29%**, compared to **11.29%** for 1-Year and **2.78%** for 2-Year plans.
- **Support Discontent:** Support person attitude contributed to **203** customer cancellations.
- **Payment Method Vulnerability:** Direct Debit ($34.49\%$) and Paper Check ($38.01\%$) users churn at more than double the rate of Credit Card users ($14.46\%$).
